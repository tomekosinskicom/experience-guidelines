## Overview

These principles apply to every product experience across the organisation. Each pillar (Sports, Gaming, Player Experience) extends these with domain-specific principles that add context but never contradict the organisation-wide set.

**Principle hierarchy:**
1. Organisation-wide principles (this document) — always apply
2. Pillar-specific principles — add domain context, may adjust priority within their pillar
3. Area-specific guidance — documented in individual experience area guidelines

## Principles in Context

_To be defined collaboratively across pillar leads and Design Ops._

Candidate organisation-wide principles (for discussion):

1. **Player Trust First** — Every interaction must maintain and build player trust through transparency, honesty, and reliability
2. **Accessible by Default** — Experiences must be usable by everyone regardless of ability, device, or context
3. **Consistent Across Journeys** — Players move between pillars (sports → casino → account) — transitions must feel seamless
4. **Protect the Player** — Responsible gambling safeguards are non-negotiable across all products
5. **Localisation is Not Translation** — Each market has cultural, regulatory, and behavioural differences that require genuine adaptation

## Current State

<!-- ai:scope -->

Organisation-wide principles are not yet formalised. Each pillar currently operates with its own principles (Sports has 6 documented). This document will be populated through a cross-pillar workshop.

## Guidelines

<!-- ai:constraints -->
- [required]: All pillar-specific principles must be compatible with organisation-wide principles (scope: all-pillars)
- [prohibited]: Pillar principles must not contradict organisation-wide principles (scope: all-pillars)
- [boundary]: Organisation-wide principles are limited to 5-7 to remain memorable and actionable (scope: this-document)
