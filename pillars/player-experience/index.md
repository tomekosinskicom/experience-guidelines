## Overview

The Player Experience pillar covers all experiences that span the player lifecycle — from first registration through ongoing account management, communications, safety, and financial transactions.

**Subdomains:**
- Onboarding — Registration, KYC, first deposit, welcome journeys
- Channels — CRM, notifications, email, push, in-app messaging
- Player Safety — Responsible gambling tools, self-exclusion, limits, reality checks
- Payments & Cashier — Deposits, withdrawals, payment methods, transaction history
