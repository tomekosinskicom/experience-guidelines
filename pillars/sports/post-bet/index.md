# Post Bet Subdomain

The Post Bet subdomain encompasses all experiences that occur after a bet has been placed — from active bet tracking and cashout through to results, settlement, and historical bet review.

<!-- ai:summary -->
Post Bet covers post-placement experiences: active bet tracking, cashout decisions, partial cashout, results delivery, settlement notifications, bet history, and re-engagement flows.
<!-- ai:keywords -->

## Scope

**Included:**
- Active bet tracking and status
- Cashout and partial cashout experiences
- Results and settlement notifications
- Bet history and review
- Win/loss communication
- Re-engagement and next-bet suggestions
- Multi-bet progress tracking

**Excluded:**
- Bet placement flows (see Transactional)
- Event discovery (see Discovery)
- Live streaming during tracking (see Cross-Cutting: Streaming and Visualisations)
