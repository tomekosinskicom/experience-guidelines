# Cross-Cutting Areas

Cross-cutting areas are experience domains that span multiple subdomains (Discovery, Transactional, Post Bet) and require coordinated design guidance across teams.

<!-- ai:summary -->
Ten cross-cutting areas influence multiple subdomains with varying degrees of impact. Each area has a scope map defining primary, secondary, and informational influence on each subdomain.
<!-- ai:keywords -->

## Areas

| Area | Description | Influenced Subdomains |
|------|-------------|----------------------|
| [Live Betting](live-betting/) | In-play betting spanning pre-match to live event transitions | Discovery, Transactional, Post Bet |
| [Horse Racing](horse-racing/) | Racing-specific experiences including form, cards, and visualisation | Discovery, Transactional, Post Bet |
| [Bet Builder](bet-builder/) | Custom bet construction combining multiple selections within events | Discovery, Transactional |
| [Personalisation](personalisation/) | Tailored content, recommendations, and adaptive interfaces | Discovery, Transactional, Post Bet |
| [Streaming and Visualisations](streaming-and-visualisations/) | Live video, match trackers, and data visualisation | Discovery, Post Bet |
| [Promotions and Boosts](promotions-and-boosts/) | Promotional offers, boosted odds, and incentive experiences | Discovery, Transactional, Post Bet |
| [Responsible Gambling](responsible-gambling/) | Player protection, limits, self-exclusion, and safer gambling | Transactional, Post Bet |
| [Localisation](localisation/) | Market-specific adaptations, language, and regulatory compliance | Discovery, Transactional, Post Bet |
| [Accessibility](accessibility/) | Inclusive design ensuring usability for all abilities and contexts | Discovery, Transactional, Post Bet |
| [Design System](design-system/) | Shared components, tokens, and interaction patterns | Discovery, Transactional, Post Bet |

## Interaction Points

Cross-cutting areas frequently overlap in their influence on subdomains. Key interaction points include:

- **Live Betting × Streaming**: Real-time visualisations during in-play betting
- **Responsible Gambling × Promotions**: Ensuring promotional offers respect player limits
- **Personalisation × Accessibility**: Adaptive interfaces that remain accessible
- **Localisation × Design System**: Component variants for market-specific requirements
- **Bet Builder × Live Betting**: Custom bet construction during live events

<!-- ai:constraints -->
- Each cross-cutting area must define influence level (primary, secondary, informational) per subdomain
- Interaction points must be documented where two or more areas overlap
- All areas must have an assigned owning Pod
