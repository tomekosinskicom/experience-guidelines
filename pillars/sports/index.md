## Overview

The Sports pillar covers all experiences related to sports betting — from discovering events and markets, through placing bets, to tracking outcomes and settlements.

**Subdomains:**
- [Discovery](discovery/) — Pre-bet browsing, search, and event exploration
- [Transactional](transactional/) — Bet placement, betslip, and payment flows
- [Post Bet](post-bet/) — Bet tracking, cashout, results, and settlements
- [Retail](retail/) — In-shop sports betting experiences
- [Tools](tools/) — Internal tools supporting sports operations

**Cross-Cutting Areas:**
- [Live Betting](cross-cutting-areas/live-betting/) — In-play experiences
- [Horse Racing](cross-cutting-areas/horse-racing/) — Racing-specific patterns
- [Bet Builder](cross-cutting-areas/bet-builder/) — Custom bet construction
- [Design System (Dice)](cross-cutting-areas/design-system/) — L2 component registry

**Principles:** See [Sports UX Principles](principles.md)

**Ownership:** See [RACI Matrix](ownership/raci-matrix.yaml) | [Designers](ownership/designers.yaml)
