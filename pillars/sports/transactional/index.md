# Transactional Subdomain

The Transactional subdomain encompasses all experiences related to the act of placing a bet — from adding selections to the betslip through to successful bet confirmation and payment processing.

<!-- ai:summary -->
Transactional covers bet placement experiences: betslip interactions, stake entry, odds acceptance, payment processing, and confirmation flows that convert intent into placed bets.
<!-- ai:keywords -->

## Scope

**Included:**
- Betslip management and interactions
- Stake entry and adjustment
- Odds changes and acceptance flows
- Payment and deposit experiences
- Bet confirmation and receipts
- Multi-bet and system bet construction
- Quick bet and one-tap placement

**Excluded:**
- Event browsing and discovery (see Discovery)
- Post-placement tracking (see Post Bet)
- Bet Builder construction (see Cross-Cutting: Bet Builder)
