## Overview

**Author:** Chunyan Ren (User Research Lead, Sports Pillar)

**Method:** 8 × 30-min qualitative interviews with high-value Ladbrokes sports bettors

**Research Question:** Should Bet Builder Odds Boosts be replaced with % Winning Boosts?

**Date:** January 2026

## Principles in Context

- **Clarity Over Cleverness** (Principle 1): Users self-discover that bigger bets = bigger boosts — this should be communicated explicitly. Stake caps and effective % uplift are unclear, undermining fairness perception.
- **Trust Through Transparency** (Principle 3): A switch to % Winning Boost risks feeling like a "PR rebrand" if not handled with genuine transparency about the value change.
- **Consistency Builds Confidence** (Principle 5): Users expect reliable boost rhythms (minimum once/week), not occasional big offers.

## Current State

<!-- ai:scope -->

The current Odds Boost mechanic drives two distinct user behaviours:

1. **Loyal users** — boost is an expected perk that nudges incremental bets once they're already on Ladbrokes
2. **Value shoppers** — boost is a price lever in cross-bookmaker comparison; they go wherever the final return is best

Clarity gaps exist: users understand bigger bets = bigger boosts, but this is self-discovered, not communicated. Stake caps and effective % uplift are unclear, undermining fairness perception and comparison ability.

## Guidelines

<!-- ai:constraints -->
- [required]: Preserve user choice in where to apply boosts — flexibility is a core value signal and Ladbrokes differentiator (scope: price-boosts)
- [required]: Show stake caps and effective % uplift at point of decision for transparency (scope: price-boosts)
- [prohibited]: Do not restrict boosts to pre-selected bets only — this risks disengagement and removes a competitive advantage vs Bet365 (scope: price-boosts)
- [boundary]: Minimum boost frequency: once per week, ideally twice (midweek European + weekend football). Daily during World Cup (scope: price-boosts)

### Key Findings

**1. Odds Boost drives behaviour in two ways:**
- Loyal users — boost is an expected perk that nudges incremental bets
- Value shoppers — boost is a price lever in cross-bookmaker comparison

**2. Clarity gaps exist:**
- Users understand bigger bets = bigger boosts, but this is self-discovered
- Stake caps and effective % uplift are unclear, undermining fairness perception

**3. Competitor advantage = "Super Sub" (not boost mechanics):**
- The #1 reason users prefer Bet365 for Bet Builders is substitution protection
- This structural risk issue outweighs any promotional mechanic
- Competitors also win on headline "Super Boosts" and personalised perks for high-spend users

**4. Reaction to % Winning Boost:**
- Upside: Feels more attractive, easier to understand, could increase BB trial among novice users
- Risks: Could feel like a "PR rebrand" (trust erosion), reduces boost-driven site visits that enable cross-sell, doesn't change value-maximiser behaviour
- Won't move the needle if "Super Sub" remains missing

**5. Frequency expectations:**
- World Cup: daily boosts expected as baseline
- Outside World Cup: minimum once/week, ideally twice
- Consistency > generosity

**6. Flexibility is a core differentiator:**
- Users strongly prefer choosing where to apply boosts vs. pre-selected bets
- This is a Ladbrokes strength vs. Bet365's restricted Super Boosts
- Restricting boosts risks disengagement

### Opportunities

| Area | Opportunity |
|------|-------------|
| Differentiation vs. simplicity | % boost is clearer but current odds boost drives site entry + cross-sell. Trade-off to manage. |
| Flexibility | Preserve user choice — it's a core value signal |
| Bet Builder adoption | Address structural risk (substitutions) not just reward format |
| Transparency | Show stake caps + effective % uplift at point of decision |
| Frequency | Design around reliable rhythms, not occasional big offers |

## Examples

**User quote (Loyal user):** "I check Ladbrokes first because I know there'll be a boost. If there isn't one, I might still bet but I'll check Bet365 first next time."

**User quote (Value shopper):** "I don't care what you call it — odds boost, percentage boost — I just want to know the final number. Show me what I'll win."

**User quote (Super Sub):** "The boost is nice but it doesn't matter if my player gets subbed off at 60 minutes. That's why I use Bet365 for Bet Builders."
